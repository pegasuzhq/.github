## Pegasuz

We build business software for small and mid-sized companies in Argentina and
Spain — a multi-tenant platform where each client gets their own database and
only the modules they need.

**Modules** — content and CMS · e-commerce and storefronts · ERP with stock,
point of sale and branches · fiscal invoicing · CRM · real-estate management ·
marketplace integrations.

Most of our work lives in private repositories. This organisation holds our
shared engineering defaults.
